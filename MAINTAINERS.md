# Maintainers

This file lists the current and emeritus maintainers of Kube-Policies.

> **Honest status.** Kube-Policies is a pre-1.0 Proof-of-Concept with a
> single maintainer. The roles and processes described here are intentionally
> lightweight but auditable. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for the
> full governance model.

### Responsibilities of active maintainers

- Review and merge pull requests, with priority on security and compliance paths
- Triage issues and respond to community questions
- Own the security-disclosure process (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))
- Maintain and update the POA&M and compliance documentation
- Execute the release process, including image signing and SBOM generation
  (once P6/P11 of the Production Readiness Plan is complete)
- Enforce and update the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- Enforce the separation-of-duties rules in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)

## How to become a maintainer

The process is described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#becoming-a-maintainer).
In short: sustained quality contributions over at least three months, nomination
by an existing maintainer, and approval by all active maintainers.
